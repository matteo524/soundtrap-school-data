Exhibit E
Many US states have a statewide student data privacy agreement that Soundtrap has already signed. Your district signs a short Exhibit E to join it, so there’s nothing new to negotiate. This is usually the fastest route.
Student Data Privacy Agreement
Soundtrap’s standard student data privacy agreement for US school districts. You can read it here.
PUB-avtal
Soundtrap’s personuppgiftsbiträdesavtal (data processing agreement), already used by many Swedish schools and municipalities.
DPA (Data Processing Agreement)
Soundtrap’s standard agreement on how we process personal data on your behalf, already used by schools and municipalities around the world. You can read it here.
