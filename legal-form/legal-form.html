To speed-up the review process, we can offer the following options:
The National Data Privacy Agreement (NDPA) is a standardized contract, created by the Student Data Privacy Consortium (SDPC) to streamline compliance with student data privacy laws (FERPA, COPPA) across U.S. school districts and EdTech vendors. If you'd like, you can sign the NDPA and email it to contracts@soundtrap.com
Use an existing Exhibit E to join an existing master NDPA or state-level agreement that Soundtrap has already signed with another district or consortium.
You'll find all the available agreements sorted by State at https://airtable.com/appTOZr2BCJNwtPfL/shrleIotBEk966u2zd If you'd like, you can sign an Exhibit E from one of the already approved agreements and send it to contracts@soundtrap.com
The Soundtrap Pub-Avtal is a standardized Data Privacy Agreement already used by many Swedish schools and municipalities. If you'd like, you can review and sign the Pub-Avtal and email it to contracts@soundtrap.com
The Soundtrap DPA is a standardized Data Privacy Agreement already used by many schools and municipalities in Europe, Norway and United Kingdom. If you'd like, you can review and sign the DPA and email it to contracts@soundtrap.com
